Expanding and factoring

Multiply every term by every term. To factor, look for two numbers that multiply to the last term and add to the middle one.

4.
Expand and simplify.
(x + 3)²
5.
Solve for x by factoring.
x² − 5x + 6 = 0
6.
Solve for x.
x/3 + 1 = 4

Derivatives

Power rule: bring the power down, then drop it by one. Chain rule: derivative of the outside, times the derivative of the inside.

7.
Find dy/dx.
y = x³ − 2x
8.
Find dy/dx.
y = (x² + 1)³
9.
Find dy/dx. Simplify your answer.
y = (2x + 1)⁴
